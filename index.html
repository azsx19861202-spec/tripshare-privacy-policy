本政策說明我們在提供本 App 服務時，會蒐集哪些資料、為什麼蒐集、保存多久、分享給誰，以及你可以如何行使你的權利。我們的做法是只蒐集提供功能所必需的資料；不需要的資料，我們一開始就不會要。

本政策適用於你透過 Google Play 下載安裝的 Android 版本本 App，以及本 App 所連線的後端服務。若你透過其他管道（例如自行建置的版本或測試版）使用本 App，仍適用本政策，但實際功能可能略有差異。

我們承諾：

  • 不販售、不出租你的個人資料。
  • 不將你的資料用於廣告投放、跨網站追蹤或建立行銷輪廓。
  • 不在你未主動輸入的情況下，讀取你的通訊錄、相簿、精確位置、麥克風或相機。
  • 不以「提供服務」以外的目的，把資料提供給無關的第三方。

Privacy Policy — TripShare (浠邊有隻鵝)

Version 1.0 · Effective 10 November 2026 · Applies to the Android app “浠邊有隻鵝” (package com.edison.rayden.ledger). This English text is provided for convenience; the Traditional Chinese version prevails.

TripShare is a shared travel-expense ledger. It helps a group of travellers record who paid for what, calculate each person’s balance, and produce the smallest set of transfers needed to settle up. The app has no sign-up and no login: a trip is identified by a random invitation code.

What we collect

  • Content you type in — trip name, trip date, settlement currency, the display names of travellers, and each expense (description, amount, currency, date, payer, participants and their shares).
  • On-device records — the invitation codes of recently opened trips and your member identifier for those trips, stored in the app’s local storage on your phone.
  • Technical logs — timestamp, IP address, request path and error messages generated by our server for reliability and abuse prevention.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for or collect your email address, phone number, contacts, photos, camera or microphone input, precise location, advertising ID, or any payment-card or bank details. The app is a calculator and a ledger — it never moves money.

How we use it

Only to (1) store your trips and expenses, (2) compute balances and settlement suggestions, (3) sync the same trip across the travellers who hold its invitation code, (4) keep the service secure and working, and (5) answer your requests. We do not sell or rent your data, do not use it for advertising or profiling, and embed no ad network, analytics or social plug-ins.

Sharing within a trip

Anyone who has a trip’s invitation code can view and edit that trip, including traveller names and amounts. Share the code or link only with your own travel companions, and contact us if a code leaks so we can help you rotate or delete the trip.

Service providers

We use a cloud hosting and managed MySQL provider to store trip data, Google Play to distribute the app (Google handles downloads and reviews under its own policy), and an email provider when you write to us. These providers act on our instructions and receive only the minimum data needed. We may disclose data if compelled by law, and will notify you where lawful and feasible.

Retention

Trip content is kept until you ask us to delete it, or until the trip has not been accessed for 24 consecutive months, after which it is deleted or anonymised. Technical logs are kept for up to 90 days. Support emails are kept for one year after the issue is resolved.

Your rights and deletion

You may ask us to access, copy, correct, restrict or delete the data relating to you, free of charge. Email azsx19861202@gmail.com with the subject “Privacy Request”, the trip name or invitation code, and the member name you used. We respond within 30 days. Because the app creates no account, there is no account to delete; deleting the app or clearing its storage removes on-device records, while server-side trip data is removed on request.

Security

All traffic uses HTTPS/TLS, database access is limited to what the features need and uses parameterised queries, and trips are protected by a 128-bit random invitation code. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your rights we will notify you as required by law.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If a child has used the app without consent, contact us and we will delete the data. Users under 18 should use the app with a guardian’s consent.

International transfers

Our servers may be located outside your country, so your data may be transferred to and stored in another region. We require providers to apply protections consistent with this policy.

Changes

We update this page when the app or the law changes, revising the version number and effective date above. Material changes will be announced in the app before they take effect.
